Who made what here

Philosophitor is an unofficial, fan-made project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the show.

This site

Philosophitor is a tool I built for myself. I noticed I could listen to an episode of Philosophize This!, nod along the whole time, and still not be able to explain the argument if you asked me about it the next day. This quizzes you on whether an idea actually stuck. It also lets you search the full text of every transcript. I built it by describing what I wanted to Claude and iterating from there, not by writing every line by hand.

Built by Sajad Faghfoor Maghrebi.
